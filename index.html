LIVE·TEAM STORES

LIVE·TEAM Stores Privacy Policy

Operated by The Team Live Services LLC

Effective date: 12 October 2026

1

Introduction

The Team Live Services LLC ("LIVE·TEAM", "we", "us", or "our") operates LIVE·TEAM Stores, an internal application designed to help authorised personnel request, track and manage equipment, tools and consumable supplies.

This Privacy Policy explains how personal information is collected, used, stored and managed when using the application.

LIVE·TEAM Stores is intended for authorised business users and is not designed as a public consumer service.

2

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use LIVE·TEAM Stores, we may process:

  • Account information: Name, email address, user identification and assigned access permissions.
  • Order information: Equipment requests, project references, requested items, quantities, dates and order-related notes.
  • Administrative activity: Order status changes, the administrator responsible for those changes and associated timestamps.
  • Device information: Device identifiers or push-notification tokens used to deliver application notifications.
  • Technical information: Information necessary for authentication, security, troubleshooting and application operation.

We ask users not to include unnecessary personal or sensitive information in order notes.

3

How We Use Information

We process information to:

  • Authenticate users and control access to the application.
  • Create, manage and track equipment and consumable requests.
  • Notify users about orders and changes in order status.
  • Maintain operational records and administrative accountability.
  • Protect the security and integrity of the application.
  • Provide technical support and troubleshoot issues.
  • Comply with applicable legal and business obligations.
4

Access to Information

LIVE·TEAM Stores is restricted to authorised users.

Access permissions depend on the user's assigned role. Operational and management administrators may access information necessary to perform their responsibilities.

Information is not made publicly available through the application.

5

Service Providers

LIVE·TEAM Stores uses third-party technology services, including Supabase for application authentication, database functionality and backend services.

Push-notification delivery may involve Apple and Google notification services, depending on the user's device.

These services process information as necessary to support application functionality, subject to their applicable privacy and security arrangements.

6

Data Security

We use technical and organisational safeguards intended to protect information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure or loss.

These measures include authenticated access, role-based permissions and database access controls.

No electronic system can guarantee absolute security.

7

Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to operate LIVE·TEAM Stores and meet legitimate business, security and legal requirements.

Order records and administrative audit history may be retained where necessary for operational accountability, recordkeeping or legal obligations.

Information that is no longer required will be deleted or anonymised where appropriate.

8

Account Deletion and Privacy Requests

Users may request deletion of their LIVE·TEAM Stores account and associated personal information by contacting:

appsliveteamuae@the.team

Upon receiving a request, LIVE·TEAM will review it and take appropriate action, which may include:

  • Disabling access to the application.
  • Deleting or anonymising personal information where appropriate.
  • Retaining order records or administrative audit information where necessary for legitimate business, security or legal purposes.

Requests will be handled in accordance with applicable legal requirements.

9

International Data Processing

Application information may be processed or stored using third-party technology infrastructure located outside the user's country.

Where applicable, LIVE·TEAM will take appropriate measures to protect personal information in accordance with relevant data-protection requirements.

10

Children's Privacy

LIVE·TEAM Stores is intended for authorised business personnel and is not directed at children.

11

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in application functionality, operational practices or applicable legal requirements.

The latest version will be made available at the published privacy-policy URL.
