About LIVE·TEAM Stores

LIVE·TEAM Stores is an internal application used by authorised personnel to request, track and manage tools, equipment and consumable supplies.

Access to the application is restricted to approved users with an active LIVE·TEAM account.

Contact Support

For technical assistance, account access, order-related enquiries or general application support, please contact the LIVE·TEAM Stores team.

Account Access

LIVE·TEAM Stores accounts are provided to authorised personnel.

If you cannot sign in, have forgotten your password or require a change to your access permissions, please contact the Stores administration team using the support email above.

Reporting an Issue

If you experience a problem with the application, please provide:

  • A description of the issue.
  • The device type (iPhone, iPad or Android).
  • The approximate date and time of the issue.
  • Any relevant order reference.

Please do not include passwords or other sensitive account credentials in support emails.

Privacy and Account Deletion
