About LIVE·TEAM Stores

LIVE·TEAM Stores is an internal application used by authorised personnel to request, track and manage tools, equipment and consumable supplies.

Access to the application is restricted to approved users with an active LIVE·TEAM account.

Contact Support

Account Access

LIVE·TEAM Stores accounts are provided to authorised personnel.

If you cannot sign in, have forgotten your password or require a change to your access permissions, please contact the Stores administration team using the support email above.

Reporting an Issue

If you experience a problem with the application, please provide:

  • A description of the issue.
  • The device type (iPhone, iPad or Android).
  • The approximate date and time of the issue.
  • Any relevant order reference.

Please do not include passwords or other sensitive account credentials in support emails.

Privacy and Account Deletion

LIVE·TEAM Stores is an internal business application operated by The Team Live Services LLC. User accounts are created and managed by authorised company administrators.

How to Request Account or Data Deletion

Current and former users of LIVE·TEAM Stores may request deletion of their account and associated personal information.

To submit a request, email:

Account and Data Deletion Requests

appsliveteamuae@the.team

Subject: LIVE·TEAM Stores - Data Deletion Request

Please identify the account concerned. We may request additional information to verify your identity before processing the request. Do not provide your password.

Account Deactivation

When an employee leaves The Team Live Services LLC, their application access may initially be disabled. This allows existing business records and order histories to remain available to authorised administrators.

Information Deleted

Following a verified deletion request, personal account information, including associated account identifiers, names and email addresses, will be reviewed for deletion or anonymisation where no longer required.

Information That May Be Retained

Certain business records may need to be retained for legitimate operational, accounting, security, audit or legal purposes.

These may include equipment requests, order histories, approval records and associated transaction information.

Personal identifiers will be removed or anonymised where feasible and where retention of identifiable information is no longer necessary.

Data Retention

Retention periods depend on the type of information and applicable business or legal requirements. Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for those purposes.

Users may contact the support address above to request further information about the retention of their personal data.
